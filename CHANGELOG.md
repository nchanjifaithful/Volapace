# Changelog

## 1.1

- **New look.** The official Volapace logo on the app icon, the launch screen and inside the app, with a short intro that explains what Volapace is.
- **Clearer setup.** Choosing a pace is followed by a short reminders step (which you can skip) and a summary of your whole plan, then a welcome animation as your plan starts.
- **Rest days.** Keep Saturdays, Sundays or both free, or none.
- **Tick pages as read.** Each page has a Mark read button, and finishing every page of today's range completes the day. Markers show where each day's reading ends, and you can keep reading past them.
- **Plan fixes.** Finishing a day now updates the plan page, its dates and the streak straight away.
- **Reader.** Smoother pinch and double-tap zoom that keeps the spot under your fingers, sharper text for PDFs without embedded fonts, a ⋮ menu for page options, the bookmark back in the toolbar, and Highlight and Note in Android's own text menu.
- **Search** highlights the match on the page and stays until you clear it, with next and previous buttons.
- **Bookmarks** on each book's plan page and across all books from the Library.
- **Library and Home.** A search bar above the filters, and a floating + button to add a book.
- **Profile.** Profile photo, a date picker for Reading since, total books and hours, a Reminders page with quiet hours and a separate streak-rescue time, Check for updates, and buttons to refresh or open the books folder.
- **Books folder.** Optionally keep books in a Volapace folder in main storage, where any file manager or a computer can reach it.
- **Progress** opens on This week, and weeks and months follow the calendar.

## 1.0

The first release of Volapace, an offline reading planner for PDFs and paper books.

- **Starts empty.** No sample books, numbers or profile: everything on screen comes from what you add and read.
- **Your books.** Pick a PDF (it's copied into the Volapace folder) or open one from another app with *Open with* or *Share*. PDFs copied into the folder over USB appear by themselves. Paper books are added by title and page count.
- **Reading plans.** Set a pace by finish date, pages a day or minutes a day; skip covers, contents and notes; keep Sundays free. Edit or reset a plan at any time.
- **Reader.** Swipe or scroll, zoom, chapters from the PDF's outline, search, bookmarks, highlights with notes, page colours, and a reading timer that pauses after 2 idle minutes.
- **Today, Progress and streaks.** Daily goal, streak calendar, pages and hours by week, month and year, a yearly book goal, and a shareable streak card.
- **Reminders.** A daily nudge and a streak rescue at 9 PM, with quiet hours. They're local notifications, with no push service.
- **Private by design.** The app has no internet permission. Export your history as CSV or JSON whenever you like.
