# Changelog

## 1.0

The first release of Volapace, an offline reading planner for PDFs and paper books.

- **Starts empty.** No sample books, numbers or profile: everything on screen comes from what you add and read.
- **Your books.** Pick a PDF (it's copied into the Volapace folder) or open one from another app with *Open with* or *Share*. PDFs copied into the folder over USB appear by themselves. Paper books are added by title and page count.
- **Reading plans.** Set a pace by finish date, pages a day or minutes a day; skip covers, contents and notes; keep Sundays free. Edit or reset a plan at any time.
- **Reader.** Swipe or scroll, zoom, chapters from the PDF's outline, search, bookmarks, highlights with notes, page colours, and a reading timer that pauses after 2 idle minutes.
- **Today, Progress and streaks.** Daily goal, streak calendar, pages and hours by week, month and year, a yearly book goal, and a shareable streak card.
- **Reminders.** A daily nudge and a streak rescue at 9 PM, with quiet hours. They're local notifications, with no push service.
- **Private by design.** The app has no internet permission. Export your history as CSV or JSON whenever you like.
